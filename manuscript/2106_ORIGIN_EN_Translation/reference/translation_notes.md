# Translation Notes

## Chapter Structure
- 35 chapters in 3 volumes
- Chinese chapter titles are poetic/abstract
- English titles should preserve mystery, not explain

## Key Challenges

### 1. ORIGIN's Voice
Chinese ORIGIN speaks in clean, numbered, logical statements.
English must avoid sounding like HAL 9000 or a chatbot.
Solution: Short sentences. No contractions. No rhetorical questions.

### 2. Scientific Concepts
Hermann's formula (CV∞) needs to be readable but credible.
Solution: Keep mathematical notation. Explain through dialogue, not narration.

### 3. Emotional Subtext
Chinese text relies heavily on what's NOT said.
English must preserve silence and implication.
Solution: Short paragraphs. White space. Unfinished thoughts.

### 4. Time Jumps
Chapter 13 jumps 18 years.
Chinese uses report-style headings.
English should use mixed media (news clips, transcripts, diary entries).

- [ ] All character names consistent with glossary
- [ ] ORIGIN never sounds emotional
- [ ] No Chinese characters in body text
- [ ] Technical terms defined on first use
- [ ] Dialogue sounds natural to native English speakers
- [ ] Chapter titles preserve original ambiguity
